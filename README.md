## Overview

***Hyrule Restoria*** is a cross-disciplinary lore encyclopedia and primary-canon audit of *The Legend of Zelda* from *Skyward Sword* through *Tears of the Kingdom*. Rather than relying on contradictory out-of-universe marketing graphics that attempt to merge incompatible continents, this work evaluates the physical architecture, sedimentary stone erosion, marine bio-erosion, freshwater hydrology, fossil strata, material relic lineages, hereditary clan genealogy, and causal physics built directly into the games.

By applying real-world archaeological methods (**Stratigraphy**, **Material Typology**, **Geoarchaeology**, **Genealogy**, and **Source Criticism**) alongside theoretical physics (**Many-Worlds Non-Convergence**, **Fixed Points in Superposition**, and **Closed Causal Loops**), *Hyrule Restoria* demonstrates that:
1. **Prime Hyrule never suffered *The Wind Waker*’s Great Sea flood.**
2. ***Breath of the Wild* and *Tears of the Kingdom* sit strictly at the end of the single, unflooded Prime Downfall Trunk** following a post-NES dark age and King Rauru’s mainland refounding.
3. **The Parallel Adult Branch (*Wind Waker* &rarr; *Spirit Tracks*) and Parallel Child Branch (*Majora’s Mask* &rarr; *Twilight Princess* &rarr; *Four Swords Adventures*)** remain completely intact as autonomous parallel universes without requiring impossible continent mergers.

## Core Analytical Pillars

* **Zero-Flood Geological, Hydrological & Genealogical Proof:** Porous sandstone monuments (Seven Heroines) older than the Great Pyramids retain razor-sharp carvings without water fissures; inland rivers and Depths basins are 100% pure freshwater with zero marine bio-erosion; prehistoric mountain Rock Salt reflects ancient geological seabeds; and an unbroken Knights of Hyrule clan genealogy endures on the mainland (whereas *The Wind Waker*’s deluge erased family trees and royal records).
* **Non-Convergence & Superposition (Laws I–III):** Parallel universes governed by Many-Worlds branching cannot physically collide their continents. Ganon unintentionally crushing the brittle crystalline *Ocarina of Time* (leaving Saria’s powerless wooden Fairy Ocarina) locked the Prime Trunk forward, while placing King Rauru’s refounding after the post-NES collapse ensures the Master Sword never duplicates itself across classic eras.
* **Dual-Reality *Majora’s Mask*:** *Ocarina of Time*’s Ganon’s Tower clash acts as a Fixed Point in Superposition launching *Majora’s Mask* across two consecutive outcomes—a pure physical journey to live on in the Child Branch (keeping *Twilight Princess*’s Hero’s Shade 100% intact) and a Sacred Realm purgatory journey in the Prime Downfall Trunk, where Skull Kid mirrors Link’s ten-year-old grief and spiritual echoes (Romani/Cremia, Tatl/Tael, and the Moon Children) guide his soul to peace.

## Epistemological Standard

Every claim in *Hyrule Restoria* is transparently categorized into two tiers:
* **Tier 1 — Direct Primary Evidence (~85%–90%):** Objective facts verified directly inside the published games (architecture, stone monuments, inventory items, dialogue, geography, and game mechanics) paired with real-world physical baselines.
* **Tier 2 — Reasoned Causal & Historical Inference (~10%–15%):** Transparent logical bridges required by cause-and-effect to connect verified primary facts across off-screen historical gaps.
