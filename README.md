## Overview

**Hyrule Restoria** is an unofficial, non-commercial archaeogaming and primary-canon research project by **xorace7701** reconstructing the chronology of *The Legend of Zelda* across all 40 years of the series—from *Skyward Sword* through *Breath of the Wild* and *Tears of the Kingdom*.

Rather than relying on contradictory out-of-universe promotional charts or forcing incompatible continents to merge, this archive evaluates the primary physical evidence built directly into the games—geology, hydrology, sedimentary stone erosion, marine bio-erosion, fossil records, living bloodlines, and causal relic mechanics—to demonstrate that **Prime Hyrule never flooded**, and that the multiverse is governed by strict physical and metaphysical laws.
