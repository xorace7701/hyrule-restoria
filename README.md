## Overview

***Hyrule Restoria*** is a cross-disciplinary lore encyclopedia auditing *The Legend of Zelda* through **Primary In-Game Canon** (physical stone architecture, stratigraphy, hydrology, inventory lineages, and gameplay mechanics) alongside real-world scientific baselines across **Geology, Hydrology, Marine Biology, Archaeology, Genealogy, and Causal Physics**.

Rather than relying on contradictory external promotional charts, this framework demonstrates why **Prime Hyrule never suffered *The Wind Waker*’s Great Flood**, anchors *Breath of the Wild* and *Tears of the Kingdom* strictly at the end of the single, unflooded **Prime Downfall Trunk**, and resolves the three-branch split through **Fixed Points in Superposition** and **Non-Convergence Laws**.

## Core Analytical Pillars

* **Zero-Flood Geological & Hydrological Proof:** East Gerudo’s porous sandstone Seven Heroines retain razor-sharp carvings after tens of thousands of years without water fissures (contrasted against Egypt’s limestone Great Sphinx), while inland rivers remain 100% pure freshwater with zero marine bio-erosion on pre-Sheikah Lomei Labyrinths.
* **Non-Convergence & Superposition (Laws I–III):** Parallel branches cannot physically merge continents. *Ocarina of Time*’s Ganon’s Tower clash acts as a **Fixed Point in Superposition**, where Ganon unintentionally shattering the brittle crystalline Ocarina of Time (while Saria’s wooden Fairy Ocarina lacked temporal magic) locked the Prime Trunk forward, and King Rauru’s refounding sits after the post-NES collapse to prevent Master Sword duplication.
* **Unbroken Hereditary Lineage & Genealogy Proof:** An unbroken collateral knightly clan lineage—from the Knights of Hyrule of the Imprisoning War to Link’s uncle in *A Link to the Past* and Royal Guard Link in *Breath of the Wild* and *Tears of the Kingdom*—proves continuous mainland survival that *The Wind Waker*’s population-scattering deluge would have erased.
* **Dual-Reality *Majora’s Mask*:** Resolves the decade-long *"Is Link dead in Majora’s Mask?"* debate without breaking *Twilight Princess*—framing Termina as one continuous soul-journey experienced simultaneously as a waking physical adventure (Child Branch) and a Sacred Realm purgatory sanctuary (Prime Downfall Trunk).

## Epistemological Standard

Every claim across this archive is transparently categorized into two scholarly tiers:
1. **Tier 1 — Direct Primary Evidence (~85%–90%):** Verified in-game monuments, architectural strata, inventory items, dialogue receipts, and established physical/scientific laws.
2. **Tier 2 — Reasoned Causal Inference (~10%–15%):** Necessary logical bridges connecting two canonical on-screen facts across an off-screen gap (such as the off-screen shattering of the crystalline Ocarina of Time or post-collapse semantic drift).
