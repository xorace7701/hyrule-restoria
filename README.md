### Core Overview

**Hyrule Restoria** is an independent, primary-canon archaeogaming and historical-methodology reconstruction of *The Legend of Zelda* multiverse. Rather than relying on contradictory out-of-universe promotional charts or forcing three incompatible timelines to merge into one landmass, this archive evaluates the primary physical evidence built directly into the games—geology, sandstone bio-erosion, freshwater hydrology, architecture, closed-loop relic physics, and living bloodlines—to demonstrate that mainland Hyrule remained continuously unflooded from *Skyward Sword* through *Tears of the Kingdom*.

---

### Author's Archival Note — Independent Parallel Discovery

At the time this framework was originally formulated and written, the author had no prior knowledge of Nintendo’s September and November 2026 official website timeline updates—arriving at King Rauru’s post-*Zelda II* Refounding placement independently through primary in-game geology, stratigraphy, and closed-loop Master Sword physics. While Nintendo’s official updates subsequently validated this exact post-classic Refounding sequence, Nintendo’s chart retained an unresolved horizontal line treating the *Wild*/*Tears* era as a vague "shared epilogue" across all three branches—leaving the physical and causal proofs established in this repository essential to demonstrate why the Refounding belongs strictly to the single unflooded mainland trunk.
