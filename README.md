## Overview

Conventional *Legend of Zelda* timeline discussions often rely on external promotional charts that treat parallel branches as though they could physically collide before *Breath of the Wild*, or frame the primary classical continuity as a hypothetical "failure."

**Hyrule Restoria** evaluates the series through **Primary In-Game Canon** (monuments, geology, architecture, dialogue, and gameplay mechanics) alongside transparent **Causal Inference**:
* **Zero Ocean Flood on the Main Continent:** Ten natural-science and archaeological proofs (stratigraphy, porous sandstone bio-erosion, prehistoric seabed rock salt, freshwater hydrology, marine biology, *The Wind Waker*'s underwater stasis-dome collapse, and missing *Spirit Tracks* railroad infrastructure) confirm that Prime Hyrule remained continuously dry across every era.
* **Three Governing Laws of the Multiverse:** Establishes **Law I (One Outcome Per Timeline / Non-Convergence)**, **Law II (The Unintentional Crushed Crystalline Ocarina Lock)**, and **Law III (One Blade Per Era / Closed Causal Loop)**.
* **Dual-Reality *Majora's Mask* & Sacred Realm Cosmology:** Resolves the "Is Link dead?" debate through simultaneous dual-outcome consciousness across parallel branches—preserving *Twilight Princess* intact while mapping the Sacred Realm's mirror law, Kokiri woodwind culture, and the lone-tree chambers.
* **Noble Sacrifice (*Mono no Aware*):** Reframes the classical timeline through traditional Japanese storytelling philosophy rather than Western wish-fulfillment.
