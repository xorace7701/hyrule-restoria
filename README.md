## Overview

**Hyrule Restoria** is a comprehensive, multi-volume primary-canon reconstruction of *The Legend of Zelda* chronology from *Skyward Sword* through *Tears of the Kingdom*. Rather than forcing three mutually contradictory timelines to magically converge into one landmass—or dismissing the original 1986–1993 classic continuity as a hypothetical "what-if" failure—this archive evaluates the franchise strictly through **Tier 1 Primary In-Game Evidence** (architecture, sandstone erosion, freshwater hydrology, marine biology, fossil records, living bloodlines, and item mechanics) paired with transparent **Tier 2 Causal Inferences** (85%–90% Tier 1 facts / 10%–15% Tier 2 synthesis).

### Core Pillars of the Framework
* **Zero-Flood Mainland Continuity:** Ten independent natural-science and archaeological proofs demonstrating that Prime Hyrule never suffered *The Wind Waker*’s Great Sea flood or underwater stasis-dome collapse—preserving porous sandstone monuments, ancient subterranean vaults, wild horses, freshwater rivers, and missing *Spirit Tracks* railroad infrastructure intact across tens of thousands of years.
* **The Three Governing Laws of the Multiverse:**
  1. **Law I — One Outcome Per Timeline (Non-Convergence):** Parallel quantum branches remain independent physical universes that never merge continents.
  2. **Law II — The Unintentional Crushed Ocarina Lock:** Ganon unintentionally shatters the brittle crystalline Ocarina of Time upon Link’s body atop Ganon’s Tower, leaving only Saria’s powerless wooden Fairy Ocarina and locking the Prime Universe onto an irreversible forward path.
  3. **Law III — One Blade Per Era (Closed Causal Loop):** The surface Master Sword rests untouched by time travel through the classic games before traveling back solely to King Rauru’s post-NES Refounding era, preventing any two-sword duplication paradox.
* **Dual-Reality *Majora’s Mask* & Sacred Realm Cosmology:** Resolves the decades-old *Majora’s Mask* debate through one continuous soul-journey branching simultaneously into a physical adventure in the Parallel Child Branch and a Sacred Realm purgatory in the Prime Trunk—where Skull Kid’s wooden flute mirrors Link’s own ten-year-old grief and the sunlit Moon meadow reveals the uncorrupted Sacred Realm sanctuary.
